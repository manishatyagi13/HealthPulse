HOW IT WORKS

Preventive health, made simpler.

DrMudhiwalla brings health screening closer to everyday places people already visit.

THE SCREENING JOURNEY

From everyday visit to health awareness.

Our approach is designed to make preventive health screening convenient and easier to understand.

01

Visit

People visit places they already use regularly, such as participating bank branches.

02

Screen

Basic health parameters and lifestyle indicators can be captured through the screening process.

03

Understand

Screening information is presented in a simple and understandable way.

04

Act

Better awareness can support more informed decisions about health.

STARTING WITH BANKS

Screening where everyday life already happens.

Our initial model explores convenient health screening at bank branches, helping people access health awareness without requiring a separate visit to a health centre.
