ABOUT DRMUDHIWALLA

Making preventive health more accessible.

DrMudhiwalla HealthTech is building infrastructure that makes health screening more convenient by bringing it closer to everyday environments.

OUR APPROACH

Healthcare should not always require a separate trip.

Preventive health awareness can become part of everyday life when people have convenient opportunities to understand basic health indicators.

We are exploring how technology and health screening infrastructure can work together across places people already visit.
